# EA DBS Diagnosis

## Goal

Determine the current bottleneck and the next highest-information action.

## Diagnose in this order

1. User: Is the user specific enough?
2. Problem: Is the pain observed or assumed?
3. Artifact: Why does a PDF improve the job?
4. Distribution: Can content reliably reach the affected users?
5. Purchase: Is there evidence of willingness to pay?
6. Usage: Can we observe whether the artifact is actually useful?
7. Repeatability: Can the winning mechanism produce a family of products?

## Output

Always return:

- current core judgment
- strongest evidence
- biggest uncertainty
- one next test
- kill / pivot condition

## Constraint

Do not recommend a course, coaching, or service merely because it is easier to monetize.
The business objective is PDF-first artifacts.
