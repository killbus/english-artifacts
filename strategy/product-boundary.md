# Product Boundary

## We are NOT building

- an English course
- a tutoring service
- a coaching business
- a conventional curriculum
- a generic English-content account
- a habit-tracker business
- a template dump
- PDFs created merely to fill pages

## We ARE exploring

- PDF-first digital products
- guided artifacts
- reflective / accumulative artifacts
- repeated personal-use tools
- artifacts with return / echo mechanisms
- small commercial experiments
- product systems that can be produced repeatedly

## Hard boundary

Technical implementation must not silently redefine product behavior.
Team C implements product intent; it does not redefine product intent.
