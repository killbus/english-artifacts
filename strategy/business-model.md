# Business Model

```text
Content
  ↓
Demand / Problem Discovery
  ↓
Low-cost PDF
  ↓
Purchase
  ↓
Usage
  ↓
Feedback / Evidence
  ↓
Next Product
```

## Initial objective

The first objective is learning, not revenue maximization.

Test whether users will pay for a tightly defined artifact that solves a
meaningful problem.

## Important distinction

Track separately:

- content interest
- download intent
- purchase
- actual use
- repeat purchase
- willingness to pay

A symbolic low price is a diagnostic experiment, not necessarily the product's
eventual value.
