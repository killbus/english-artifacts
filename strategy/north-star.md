# North Star

## Ultimate objective

Build a repeatable system for discovering, validating, and selling PDF-first
digital products.

## Channel

Content channels such as Xiaohongshu are distribution, demand-discovery,
and experimentation layers. They are not the business itself.

## Product

The PDF is the product.

The artifact may be a journal, workbook, toolkit, worksheet, card system,
reference artifact, reflective artifact, or another format.

Format must follow user need.
