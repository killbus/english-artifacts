# Theory Grounding

Theory explains observed behavior and generates stronger product hypotheses.
It must not be used as decoration.

## Candidate areas

- agenda setting
- language learning behavior
- retrieval and recall
- self-monitoring
- learner autonomy
- language ownership
- memory / repetition
- behavioral friction
- product / pricing psychology

## Record for every theory used

1. observed phenomenon
2. theoretical explanation
3. product implication
4. evidence that could falsify the implication
