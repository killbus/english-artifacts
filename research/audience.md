# Audience Research

Do not start with demographic labels alone.

Start from a repeated problem or task.

## Candidate segment

18–25 year-old Chinese English learners with meaningful prior English study
but visible friction in real-world use.

This is a hypothesis, not a settled audience definition.

## Questions

- Who experiences the problem?
- In what situation?
- How often?
- What makes the problem costly or annoying?
- What do they currently do?
- Who decides to pay?
- What evidence would make this segment attractive?
