# Source Capture Checklist

Before recording evidence:

- [ ] Capture the exact source URL.
- [ ] Record the date checked.
- [ ] Separate observed facts from interpretation.
- [ ] Preserve short user wording where appropriate.
- [ ] Record licensing / reuse constraints.
- [ ] Do not treat views, likes, or saves as purchase evidence.
- [ ] Do not infer demand from a single anecdote.
