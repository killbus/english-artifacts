# Content ↔ Product Map

This file defines the relationship between distribution content and paid artifacts.

## Core loop

```text
Real user problem
      ↓
Agenda / problem recognition
      ↓
Content interaction
      ↓
Evidence of demand
      ↓
Artifact hypothesis
      ↓
Purchase / use
      ↓
Feedback
      ↓
New problem evidence
```

## Content is not the product

A post may explain a mechanism, create a question, demonstrate a problem, or show a before / after.

It does not need to teach the entire subject.

## Content job

Every product-linked piece of content should answer:

1. What problem does this make visible?
2. Who recognizes themselves in it?
3. What new question or desire does it create?
4. Why is an artifact relevant to that problem?

## Product job

The artifact should answer:

1. What does the buyer do with it?
2. What does the artifact make easier, clearer, or more reusable?
3. Why is a persistent PDF useful here?
4. What evidence will tell us whether the artifact actually worked?

## Anti-patterns

Do not:

- use content only to accumulate followers;
- append a PDF to a post with no relationship to the post's problem;
- force every content topic to sell a product;
- treat comments as proof of purchase intent without testing a transaction;
- turn every post into a lesson.
